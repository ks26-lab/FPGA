Instructions for evaluators:
1. Connect EDGE Zynq Z7010 board via USB-UART and Micro-USB JTAG.
2. Program bitstream: `maze_top.bit` (or unified `multi_scene_top.bit`).
3. Launch Python CLI: `python3 maze_runner_cli.py` or `./run_cli.sh`.
4. Enter: `scene maze`.
5. Enter: `start` to begin the 5-second maze memorization phase.
6. Observe physical board diagnostic LEDs (M14..J16) reflecting states:
   - LED[3:0]: 0=IDLE, 1=DISPLAY, 3=GAMEPLAY, 4=WIN, 5=GAME_OVER
   - LED 4: Collision against wall
   - LED 5: Goal reached indicator
   - LED 6: Maze visibility strobe
7. Use arrow keys or `up`, `down`, `left`, `right` to navigate through the maze.
