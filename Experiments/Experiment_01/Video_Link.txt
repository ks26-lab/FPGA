Instructions for evaluators:
1. Connect EDGE Zynq Z7010 board via USB-UART and Micro-USB JTAG.
2. Open serial terminal (PuTTY/minicom) at 115200 baud, 8-N-1.
3. Program bitstream: `underwater_probe_top.bit`.
4. Launch CLI: `python3 underwater_probe_cli.py --hw`.
5. Observe physical board diagnostic LEDs (M14..J16) reflecting probe states:
   - LED[3:0]: FSM State (0=IDLE, 1=MOVE, 3=COLLISION, 5=RECOVERY, 6=REACHED)
   - LED 4: Collision strobe pulse
   - LED 5: Recovery active
   - LED 6: Target reached indicator
   - Pin J15: Ballast pump PWM modulation
