Instructions for evaluators:
1. Connect EDGE Zynq Z7010 board via USB-UART and Micro-USB JTAG.
2. Program bitstream: `traffic_top.bit` (or unified `multi_scene_top.bit`).
3. Launch Python CLI: `python3 main.py` or `./run_cli.sh`.
4. Enter: `scene traffic`.
5. Observe physical board diagnostic LEDs (M14..J16) reflecting signal states:
   - LED[3:0]: 0=NS_G, 1=NS_Y, 2=ALL_RED, 3=EW_G, 4=EW_Y, 5=PED, 6=EMG
   - Header L14..V13: External traffic light signals (Green, Yellow, Red for NS and EW).
6. Enter `emg north` to trigger emergency vehicle preemption and arterial wave.
